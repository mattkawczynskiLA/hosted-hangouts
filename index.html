How Hosted Hangouts Work

1. Pitch an Activity

Got a talent, kitchen, pool, or favorite spot? Pick an activity, choose how many spots you can host, and set a donation price.

2. Sign Up & Pay

Parents browse, see which other school families are attending, and book spots using Credit Card or direct school Venmo.

3. 100% Back to HSH

Hosts generously donate their time and supplies so every cent goes to financial aid and tuition assistance for our students.
